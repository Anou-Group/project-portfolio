1. Purpose

This plan sets out how the Scrubness website (scrubness.com) will be used to bring in business, what it costs to run and grow, my plan for the site as developer, and how results will be measured.

3. What the platform is for

  • Online store for Scrubness medical scrubs: women's scrub tops, men's scrub tops and scrub pants.
  • 2026 range on sale: 22 colour products in 7 styles (Anna, Alex, Parker, Joggeroo, Ellen, Charlie and Joey).
  • Prices: $49.95 for Anna, Alex, Parker and Joggeroo, and $54.95 for Ellen, Charlie and Joey. Prices are shown excl. GST and the cart adds 10% GST.
  • Payment through Stripe: card, Afterpay, Klarna and BECS direct debit are offered at checkout.
  • Contact page with an enquiry form, phone 1800 870 858, address 236 Proximity Dr, Sunshine West VIC 3020, email hello@scrubness.com and hours Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm AEDT.
  • Mailchimp for WooCommerce is installed, which connects store customers to Mailchimp email marketing.
  • Built on WordPress and WooCommerce, on cPanel hosting with Cloudflare in front of the site.

6.2 After go-live: search set-up

  • Set up the free trial of Ahrefs or Semrush, run a site crawl and do keyword research.
  • Write search titles and descriptions for the Shop page, 3 categories, 22 products, Terms, Privacy, Returns and Contact. These are missing now.
  • Add search terms such as "scrub top" to product page titles.
  • Add alt text to the images on the Home, About, FAQ, Privacy and Contact pages.
  • Connect the store to Google Merchant Center for free product listings.
  • Add a security plugin: login protection, two-step login and hiding WordPress information that should not be public.

6.3 Every month

  • Send a one-page results report (section 7).
  • Write blog posts on topics found through keyword research.
  • Improve page speed, starting with the Shop page, and re-check speed after any design change.
  • Apply WordPress, plugin and PHP updates, testing on the local copy first.
  • Set up new products with titles, descriptions, alt text and correct sizes from the start.
  • Fix any errors shown in Search Console.
