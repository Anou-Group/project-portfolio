The Shopify store is built on a preview theme with 158 products imported. Launch depends on business inputs: payments verification, shipping rates, return rules, stock counts and SKUs, and a go-live date.

Launch on the Shopify Grow plan already recommended, use free Google and Meta tools first, fix product data (SKUs and stock) so product feeds work, then run a three-month paid advertising test with clear stop/go measures.

1. Business overview

Confirmed Southern Uniforms is a family-owned uniform business that supplies uniforms across corporate, healthcare and aged care, education, hospitality, trade and other industries. It offers consultation and design, supply, customisation (embroidery and printing) and ongoing uniform management, including online ordering, staff allocations, reporting, and returns and replacements for organisations.

Confirmed The online store is moving from Botble (Laravel) to Shopify on the Grow plan with a custom theme built from the approved Figma design. The Shopify storefront currently lists scrubs, tunics, shirts and stretch jeans from Scrubness and City Collection, and an Ezylin collection, organised by industry: Health & Aged Care, Corporate, Hospitality, Education, Trade & Safety, Spa & Wellbeing and Beauty.

Current Phase 1 (launch foundations) is in progress. Phase 2 (growth and automation) and Phase 3 (scale and loyalty) are a starting list only and are not yet agreed.

8. Initial digital marketing strategy

None of the activities below are running. They are a proposed plan for after launch, to be approved before any spend.

Search engine optimisation (SEO)

  • Current Page titles, descriptions, sitemap and Search Console are in progress; structured data for breadcrumbs and site search is in the theme.
  • Proposal Complete 301 redirects from every current URL before the domain switch, to keep existing rankings.
  • Proposal Build each industry and product-type collection as a landing page with a short, useful introduction (for example "Aged care uniforms", "Scrub pants", "Chef wear").
  • Proposal Use the words customers search for. Keyword research (Google Trends and autocomplete, Australia, 30 Oct 2026) shows "scrub pants" has about twice the search interest of "scrub tops", and customers search "work pants women" and "work trousers women" rather than "work jeans".

Google Ads

  • Proposal Start with Shopping/Performance Max on the best-stocked ranges, plus a small search campaign for wholesale and team-order terms that lead to the quote form.
  • Proposal Track both online sales and quote requests as conversions so the B2B value of advertising is visible.

Social media, Instagram and Facebook

  • Pending confirmation Which social accounts exist and who manages them.
  • Proposal Two to three posts a week across Instagram and Facebook on four themes: uniforms by industry, real teams in uniform (with permission), new ranges and restocks, and how branded team orders work.
  • Proposal Link the accounts in the store's brand settings and footer.

Meta Ads

  • Proposal Retarget store visitors and abandoned carts first (lowest cost per sale), then test prospecting by job interest (healthcare, hospitality, beauty) within the capped budget above.

Content marketing

  • Proposal Short guides that answer buyers' questions: choosing uniforms for an aged-care team, how logo embroidery works and lead times, size and fit guides, fabric care. Each guide links to the matching collection and the quote form.
  • Pending confirmation Whether the current site's blog posts move to Shopify (decision with Ashwin).

Customer acquisition and retention

  • Proposal Email: welcome message for newsletter sign-ups, abandoned-checkout reminders, and the monthly "new ranges and restocks" email already promised on the sign-up form.
  • Proposal B2B: a simple outreach list of local clinics, aged-care providers, cafés and schools, with the Wholesale and Supply contract pages as the destination.
  • Proposal Google Merchant Center free listings as soon as SKUs and stock are complete.
